# Product maturity is a life arc, not a season

We model product maturity — POC, MVP, mature, maintenance — as **growth stage**: a one-way arc that runs once per plant. Not as a **season**. A season is external, cyclical, and shared by everything on the plot; it recurs every year regardless of what any individual plant is doing. Maturity does not recur: you never return to POC next spring. Calling POC a season implies that maintenance leads back to exploration, which is false, and that the product's phase is set by the calendar rather than by the plant.

**Consequences:** *season* is parked as calendar language only (quarters, release trains, freezes) and may never describe maturity. Three rules follow from the arc and are binding: tending is stage-dependent; the germination gate is stage-dependent, so a seed must declare the stage it germinates at; and seed saving is bound to senescence rather than harvest, so the bank cannot propagate unproven genetics.

**Considered options:** seasons as maturity phases (rejected: cyclical, implies recurrence); no maturity model at all (rejected: tending depends on the stage, so the assistant cannot dispatch correctly without one).
